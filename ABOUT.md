# About Presence Guard

Presence Guard is a quiet, on-device face-presence guard. It keeps your phone
awake while you look at it, locks it when you walk away, and locks it at once
for a stranger. No video ever leaves your phone.

Made by [Akshat Kushwaha](https://a3ro.dev) — AI systems engineer. I train
models, build the retrieval under them, and make the whole thing fast enough
to ship: [Qenlo](https://github.com/a3ro-dev/qenlo), a vector database in
Rust, and [Reflex](https://github.com/a3ro-dev/reflex-2048), a transformer
that plays 2048. Contact through [a3ro.dev](https://a3ro.dev).
